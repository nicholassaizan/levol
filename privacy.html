Levol Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Levol (the "App") is made and published by Nick Saizan, an individual developer ("I", "me"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the App.

The short version

I don't collect any personal data through the App. The App works entirely on your iPhone. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code, and it doesn't connect to the internet. Nothing you enter in the App is sent to me or to anyone else.

What the App stores, and where

To work, the App saves a few things on your device only:

  • Presets you save, such as bike type, rider and bike weight, tire width, and rim and tire setup. These are stored in a file in the App's private storage on your device.
  • Your settings, such as light or dark appearance, the last bike type you chose, and the fact that you've accepted the App's first-launch safety notice. These are stored in the App's private settings on your device.

I can't see or access any of this. It stays on your device until you delete it.

Device backups

If you back up your device with iCloud Backup or to a computer, the backup includes the App's saved presets and settings, as it does for most apps. Apple handles those backups under its own terms and privacy policy (or they're stored on your own computer). I can't access them. Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

How to delete your data

  • Delete individual presets inside the App.
  • Delete the App to remove all of its data from your device.
  • Data in an existing iCloud or computer backup stays there until that backup is deleted or replaced. You can manage iCloud backups in iOS Settings.

Because I never receive your App data, there's nothing for me to delete or hand over on request, and no consent for you to withdraw. You're always in control of the data on your device.

Information Apple may share with me

The App is distributed through Apple's App Store. Apple handles your download under its own privacy policy and gives developers download statistics that don't identify you.

If you've turned on Share With App Developers in iOS Settings (Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with me. Apple describes this information as not identifying you personally (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/app-analytics/). I use it only to fix bugs and improve the App. You can turn it off at any time on the same Settings screen.

If you contact me

If you email me, I receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. I use it only to reply to you and to deal with your question, complaint or claim. I keep support emails for up to 2 years after our last exchange and then delete them, unless the law requires me to keep them longer. I don't sell or share them, except with my email provider, which stores them on my behalf, or where the law requires. You can ask me to delete your emails sooner at nicholas.saizan@gmail.com.

No tracking, no selling, no sharing

I don't track you across other companies' apps or websites, I don't sell or "share" personal information (as California law defines those terms), and I don't use your information for advertising. Because the App collects no personal data, there's nothing to share with third parties.

Children

The App doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Because the App involves cycling safety, children should use it with a parent or guardian.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example, in the European Union, the United Kingdom or California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information held about you. I don't hold any personal data from the App. For any email you've sent me, contact nicholas.saizan@gmail.com and I'll respond as the law requires. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Your App data stays in the App's private storage on your device and is protected by iOS security features such as your device passcode and encryption. Please keep your device secure.

Changes to this policy

If I change how the App handles information, I'll update this policy and the effective date above. If a change means the App starts collecting personal data, I'll tell you in the App or in the App Store release notes before that happens, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
